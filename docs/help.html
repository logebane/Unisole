QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a pair of shoes?

Open Shoes to browse the collection. Use the available filters to narrow down the options.

How do I save shoes for later?

Select the heart on a shoe to add it to your favorites. Visit Favorites to see your saved items.

Where can I review my bag?

Open Your bag to review the items you've added and see the displayed subtotal, delivery estimate, and total.

Can I change the quantity of an item?

On the Favorites page, use the plus and minus controls beside an item to choose a quantity before adding it to your bag.
